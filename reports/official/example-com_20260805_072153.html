How This Audit Works

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is the practice of making your business visible inside AI search answers — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Google AI Overviews. Unlike traditional SEO (which optimizes for Google's link-based rankings), GEO optimizes for AI engines that choose and cite sources by different rules.

This audit checks five pillars of AI search visibility:

  • AI Crawlability (15%) — Can AI engines even reach your site? Checks robots.txt for blocked AI bots (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, etc.), sitemap presence, and indexability. This is the #1 silent killer — many businesses block AI crawlers without knowing it.
  • Content Answerability (20%) — Does your content answer questions the way AI engines extract and cite? Uses an LLM to evaluate direct answers, statistical richness, citation quality, structure, and conversational match. The GEO research benchmark showed these content strategies lift citation rates 30-40%.
  • Structured Data (15%) — Can machines parse and understand your content? Checks schema.org JSON-LD validity and coverage, title/meta tags, semantic HTML, and Open Graph.
  • AI Brand Recall (25%) — Do AI engines actually know and mention your business? We send real questions to an AI model ("What are the best [category] platforms?", "Compare [brand] vs competitors") and measure whether your business is mentioned. This tests live search/retrieval visibility — the closest proxy to "will ChatGPT/Perplexity mention me?"
  • AI Visibility & Competitive Intelligence (25%) — The most important pillar. If the AI can't find you, describe you wrong, or your competitor out-coverage you — every other pillar is wasted effort. Five sub-checks (20 pts each):
    • Platform Coverage — Does your brand exist on the 7 platforms AI engines search first (Wikipedia, GitHub, YouTube, Reddit, Crunchbase, G2, LinkedIn)?
    • Competitive Coverage Gap — Where do competitors have platform presence you don't? If a competitor is on YouTube and G2 but you're only on G2, they win that surface.
    • AI Positioning Accuracy — Does the AI describe you correctly? If you sell "premium soaps" but the AI says "affordable soaps", it recommends you for the wrong queries.
    • Share of Voice — Across category and comparison queries, does the AI mention you more or less often than your competitors?
    • Vector Search Inclusion — Will AI vector/semantic search actually retrieve your content? Measures embedding similarity between your homepage and typical user queries.

About the LLM judge: The content answerability pillar uses a large language model to assess content the same way an AI search engine would. This is a proxy for real engine behavior — no tool can perfectly predict what ChatGPT or Perplexity will cite. The scores are relative indicators of readiness, not guarantees.

Limitations: This audit covers 3-5 pages per site. Backlink analysis and live citation tracking are not included (they require paid APIs). See the README for the full methodology and reasoning.
